Artificial Inteligence - A hot take

Tools of the future

AI is a good tool, but not a good developer. However, simply denying AI doesn't make us good developers either. AI is useful for automating and speeding up processes, but I believe a human developer in charge remains the most important element. I use LLMs like Claude and Gemini as tools to generate boilerplate functions and classes. Yet it's common to see codebases today where developers simply paste whatever AI produces as long as it works (I am also Guilty of this), which leads to a much more time-consuming review process, since most AI-generated codebases contain repeated code. The balance of human intelligence and artificial intelligence is still the best combination.
